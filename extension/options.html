Sakshi Settings

Mindful attention configuration & data controls

Privacy & Data Sovereignty

Sakshi is built on the philosophy of the impartial witness. All data stays on your device. There are no analytics trackers, no screenshots, and no screen recordings. The extension strictly records the active tab URL and page title to your local storage (chrome.storage.local). If you provide a Gemini API key, only the URL and page title of ambiguous pages are transmitted to Google's Gemini endpoint to classify relevance.

Gemini Flash AI Classification

Uses gemini-2.0-flash for ambiguous pages and YouTube educational vs entertainment detection. If not set, Sakshi defaults to static rule lists.

Attention Grace Period & Demo Mode

A gentle nudge is only sent if you stay continuously on distracting content longer than this period. Default is 10 min.
When Demo Mode is on, effective grace is 10s (or custom seconds), and cooldown is reduced from 15 min to 60 sec so the demo can be easily repeated.
When you choose "Return to work", the distracting website is closed and turned off until your focus session ends.

Custom Domain Classifications

One domain per line. Subdomains are automatically included.
Shorts & Reels paths are automatically recognized as distracting.

Web Dashboard Origin

The web dashboard connects to Sakshi via the window.postMessage bridge.

Storage Management

Clear all session logs & caches

Deletes all recorded timelines, past sessions, and classification cache from this browser.
